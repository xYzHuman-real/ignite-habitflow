# Ignite HabitFlow Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 13 September 2026

Ignite HabitFlow is designed as a local-first productivity application.

## Information stored

The app stores productivity information that you enter or create, such as tasks, task settings, habits, habit completion history, focus-session history, and app preferences.

## Where information is stored

The current version stores this information locally on the Android device using Android application storage. The current version does not require an account or a cloud-sync service.

## Information sharing

The current version does not intentionally send the stored productivity data to a remote server or sell it to third parties.

## Purchases

Premium billing is not enabled in the current development build. If Google Play Billing is added in a future release, the policy will be updated to explain the relevant purchase and billing data flows before release.

## Data deletion

Because the current version is local-first, removing the app's local data or uninstalling the app removes the locally stored productivity information, subject to Android's normal backup/device behavior.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's data practices or services change. The published policy date will be updated with material changes.

## Contact

A public support/contact address will be added before the Google Play production release.
